The double-counted incentives

Problem
Production numbers didn't match, and the figures fed incentive allocation.
Cause
A dimension table with duplicate entries caused a join fan-out that double-counted rows.
Fix
Corrected the join logic at the source.
Safeguard
Built a permanent, automated validation check.
